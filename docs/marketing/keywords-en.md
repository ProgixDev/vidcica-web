# Keyword map — English (US + Canada)

**Date:** 17 September 2026 · **Markets:** United States, English Canada
**Companion to** `mots-cles.md` (French: France, Quebec, Belgium, Switzerland).

Target pages are the `/en` URLs shipped on 17 September (`/en`, `/en/tarifs`,
`/en/faq`, `/en/fonctionnalites`, `/en/cas-usage/<trade>`). Slugs stay French —
one slug per page keeps the FR/EN pair on the same route, and URL words are a
negligible ranking factor next to the content.

---

## Read this before picking targets

**English is not French with different words, and it is a much harder fight.**

- The French head terms ("générateur de vidéo IA") are contested by a handful of
  players. The English equivalent ("AI video generator") is contested by funded
  companies — InVideo, Pictory, OpusClip, Submagic, Fliki, Creatify — several of
  whom spend on content full-time. A new site does not rank there this year.
- So the English plan inverts the French one: **win the long tail and the
  industry pages first**, and treat the head terms as something you grow into
  once the site has authority.
- **Priorities below are set by winnability, not by volume.** P1 means "we can
  realistically rank for this within months". The big-volume terms sit at P3 —
  worth tracking, not worth building a page for yet.
- US and Canada share the same searches. There is no reason to build separate
  pages for them; that would split signals for no gain.

> **Volumes are not estimated here.** They need a paid tool (Ahrefs, Semrush).
> The competitiveness notes come from who currently occupies these results, not
> from a volume number.

## Where to start, in order

1. **Rewrite the four `/en/cas-usage` pages around their English keywords.** They
   are currently translations of the French copy. An American searching "real
   estate video marketing" wants different wording than a French agent. Same
   pages, English-first copy.
2. **Build `/en/alternatives/<competitor>`** — highest intent, and nothing exists
   yet. Confirm the real competitor list with the client first.
3. **Blog the P1 long-tail questions**, English first, then adapt to French.
4. **Only then** consider the head terms.

## Practical notes

- **Currency.** The pricing page shows euros. A US or Canadian visitor comparing
  plans sees a price they cannot evaluate, plus an FX fee on their card. This
  undercuts every English page that leads to `/en/tarifs`.
- **US and Canada need no separate pages.** Same keywords, same content; the
  `en` hreflang already covers both.
- **Instagram and Facebook keywords stay parked** until Meta verification clears.
- Refine all of this with Search Console data after 4–6 weeks: it reports the
  queries that actually reached the site, per country.
